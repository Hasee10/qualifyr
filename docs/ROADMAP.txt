QUALIFYR — ROADMAP (all phases, in order)
=========================================
Everything free, local-first, deterministic. Each phase is independently
shippable and ends with tests + a live check.

Recommended order: A -> B -> C -> D -> E -> F.
A and B first because they change outcomes for the emails already going out;
C only once a second mailbox exists; D and E as volume grows.


PHASE A — PROTECT THE SENDER  (~1/2 day)
----------------------------------------
The Gmail account is the single point of failure; one burn stops everything.

* Warm-up ramp: daily cap starts at ~5 and climbs to 40 over ~3 weeks per
  mailbox (configurable schedule in config/outreach/settings.yaml), instead of
  40 flat from day one.
* Send-time jitter: random spacing (e.g. 30–120 s) instead of a fixed 45 s.
* OAuth2 Gmail sending (XOAUTH2 via smtplib, or Gmail API) so no App Password
  sits in secrets; App Password stays as fallback.
* Reply-rate & bounce-rate guard: auto-pause a mailbox if bounces exceed a
  threshold in a day.


PHASE B — BETTER CONTACTS  (~1 day)
-----------------------------------
Moves reply rate more than any copy change.

* Reacher (check-if-email-exists) self-hosted in Docker -> email status becomes
  deliverable / risky / invalid instead of just MX-valid; outreach gate uses it.
* Phone type: Pakistani mobile (03xx / +92 3xx) vs landline; mobile scores
  higher in contact quality and appears in the UI.
* Explicit enrichment waterfall with provenance:
  OSM tag -> Overture -> mailto -> contact page -> visible freemail,
  recording which source gave each field (shown in the lead detail).


PHASE C — VOLUME WITHOUT SPAM  (~1/2 day)
-----------------------------------------
* Multi-inbox rotation: 2–3 Gmail accounts, per-inbox daily caps and their own
  warm-up state; the sequencer round-robins and threads follow-ups from the
  same inbox that sent Email 1.
* Ledger and reply sync become per-mailbox.


PHASE D — SIGNALS & SOURCES  (1–2 days)
---------------------------------------
* Site-quality signals (copyright year, mobile-friendly, page weight, HTTPS)
  into company-quality scoring.
* Domain age via WHOIS/RDAP (free) as a "new business" signal.
* News/RSS mention check (Google News RSS, free) as a buying signal and
  personalisation hook.
* SECP company search + chamber directories (ICCI / LCCI / KCCI) as new
  DiscoverySource adapters — buyer-heavy Pakistani lists with legal names and
  incorporation dates; Scrapy or httpx spiders per site.


PHASE E — REPLY INTELLIGENCE  (~1/2 day)
----------------------------------------
* Rule-based reply classification: interested / not interested /
  out-of-office / wrong person (with forwarded address) / unsubscribe.
  OOO pauses and retries instead of stopping; "wrong person" surfaces the
  referred contact for approval.
* Tags visible in Outreach -> Activity and on the lead.


PHASE F — SHARING & POLISH  (~1/2 day)
--------------------------------------
* One-way Google Sheets export of qualified leads (free tier, Sheets API) for
  anyone who won't open the UI.
* Static demo / landing page on GitHub Pages that emails can link to.
* UI touches: suppression list view, per-mailbox health panel, campaign YAML
  editor.


ONGOING / GATED ON YOU
----------------------
* Your real ICP + offer -> real campaign YAML (blocks nothing above, but
